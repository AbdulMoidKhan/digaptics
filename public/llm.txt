## Overview
Digaptics is a full-service digital agency operating in Chicago and serving clients nationwide across the United States. We unify seven core disciplines under one strategic roof: Digital Marketing, Web & Custom Software Development, Printing & Physical Collateral, Photography & Videography, Business Process Outsourcing (BPO), Industry-Specific Solutions, and Enterprise Ecommerce Management.

## Core Services & Families
- Digital Marketing: SEO Audits, PPC & Paid Media Management, Social Media Management, Content Strategy, Performance Marketing, Digital Reputation Management.
- Web & App Development: Custom Web Applications, React & Next.js Platforms, WordPress & Headless CMS, iOS & Android Mobile Apps, UI/UX Product Design, API Engineering.
- Commercial Printing: Premium Business Cards, Large Format Signs & Banners, Marketing Collateral, Custom Packaging, Trade Show Displays & Booth Branding.
- Photo & Videography: Commercial Brand Photography, Corporate Headshots, Product Photography, Promotional Videos, Social Media Reel Production.
- Business Process Outsourcing (BPO): Customer Support Teams, Back-Office Administrative Operations, Data Entry, Dedicated Technical Support Staff.
- Ecommerce Solutions: Shopify & WooCommerce Development, Amazon Store Management, Multi-Channel Marketplace Synchronization, Conversion Rate Optimization.
- Industry Solutions: Healthcare, Legal, Coworking, Professional Services, Real Estate, Automotive, and Hospitality.
